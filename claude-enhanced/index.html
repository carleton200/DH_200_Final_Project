            How to play

            Get out alive

            You start on the top floor of St. Lazarus General. Every floor has a handful of hallway stretches, then a stairwell. Reach the ground floor and get through the crowd at the doors.

            Your vitals

            • HP: hit 0 and you join the horde.
            • Bleeding drains HP every turn. Bandages and implants slow it.
            • Infection is the chance each turn that a fever knocks you down. Bites raise it. Antibiotics lower it.
            • Attack lowers the damage you take in a fight. Mobility raises your odds of escaping.

            Biometrics

            You cannot see your own vitals. Until you install a bionic eye or bionic arm, the monitor shows no signal and you have to go by feel. With biometrics online you get exact vitals, exact readings of every change, and a scanner read on each fight.

            Your conscience

            The hospital remembers how you treat people. Help others and survivors may return the favor. Abandon them and the dead may follow their screams to you.

            The horde

            Every turn the hospital fills up a little more and groups of zombies get bigger. Running away is often safe, but the zombies you leave alive keep hunting you and grow the horde. Fighting costs HP now; fleeing can cost more later.

            Taking time

            Moving on, searching a room and performing surgery each take a turn, and bleeding, a failing heart and infection all hit on every turn. Using an item from your pockets is free.

            Controls

            Click or tap a choice, or press 1–9. Use items with B bandage, A antibiotics, P painkillers. Esc closes this window. Your run saves after every move.
